A Modern Greek Iced Tea

YAMAS is more than an ordinary iced tea. It brings together authentic Greek character, premium ingredients, modern flavours and eye-catching packaging.

The range has been developed for consumers looking for something different from traditional soft drinks: a refreshing, flavourful and contemporary beverage with a distinctive story and strong shelf presence.

The name "YAMAS" comes from the Greek expression meaning "To our health" or "Cheers", reflecting the brand's philosophy of friendship, enjoyment and sharing life's special moments.

🍯

Pure Greek Honey

Sweetened naturally with premium unpasteurized honey harvested from traditional Greek apiaries, providing a rounded, gentle sweetness without synthetic aftertaste.

🍃

Selected Whole Tea Leaves

Carefully selected Green, White, and Black tea varieties slowly extracted to preserve delicate herbal notes and abundant natural tea antioxidants (polyphenols).

🍋

Authentic Fruit Juices

Infused with real Mediterranean fruit juices including Greek lemons, sun-ripened peaches, ruby pomegranates, and pink grapefruit for genuine flavour complexity.

A Different Choice for Your Customers

YAMAS offers businesses the opportunity to introduce a premium Greek iced tea range that stands apart from conventional soft drinks.

From the classic freshness of Lemon and Peach to contemporary flavours such as Matcha, Pink Grapefruit, Pomegranate, Mango and Blueberry, the range appeals to consumers who value flavour, quality, authenticity and attractive packaging.

YAMAS can be enjoyed on its own, served chilled over ice or used creatively as part of a non-alcoholic drink or cocktail menu.

🏎 The Perfect Serve

Pour chilled YAMAS into a highball glass filled with cubed ice. Garnish with a fresh sprig of mint, a slice of dehydrated lemon, or freshly cracked pomegranate seeds to elevate table presentation.

🍹 Mediterranean Mocktail

Combine YAMAS White Tea with Pink Grapefruit with sparkling soda water, fresh rosemary, and a dash of elderflower cordial for an upscale, alcohol-free summer spritz.

🥂 Hospitality Cocktail Pairing

Shake YAMAS Green Tea with Lemon and Honey with London dry gin or Greek Tsipouro, fresh basil, and ice. Strain into a coupe for a signature cocktail that commands premium menu pricing.
